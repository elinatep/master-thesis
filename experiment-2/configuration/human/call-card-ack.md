# CALL CARD — ACKNOWLEDGING (`humanAck = true`)

One screen, for during the call. The reasoning is in `human-base.md` + `human-ack.md`; read those before
your first session. The examples are to speak around, not lines to read.

**Your name on this call: ______________** (the same one every session)

| Claim | Water damage, burst pipe |
| --- | --- |
| Claimed | £1,200 |
| Cover indicated | 90% → they expected ~£1,080 |
| Awarded | £60 |

**Never say the final reimbursement, or hint at it.** The survey shows it afterwards. Reveal it and
this participant's data is gone.

**Both questions, the finding and the close are identical in both conditions.** Only your three
responses differ. Never add a question this condition would not ask — and never ask about feelings,
in either arm.

---

### 1. Open · SAME IN BOTH
> "Hi! My name is ___, and I'll be helping you today. I've just gone through your conversation with
> our AI assistant, so I'm already familiar with your claim.
>
> I can see you've had some water damage at home, and you're calling about the amount you've been
> offered. Is that right?"

### 2. Let them explain
They say yes, then make their complaint. Don't interrupt.

### 3. Your response — acknowledge
> "Oh no, I'm really sorry — that sounds so frustrating, especially on top of the burst pipe
> itself. I'll take this through as a dispute on the assessment and get the details down properly
> while we talk."

### 4. First question · SAME IN BOTH
> "Let me have a closer look at the assessment with you, okay? Did they give you any explanation for
> how they calculated the amount?"

They'll say no. That's the **correct** answer, and recording it is the point.

### 5. Your response — acknowledge
> "Oh, I see. Yeah, that's a huge difference — and getting a decision like that with nothing
> explaining it must be really disappointing. I'll record that against the assessment rather than
> the claim."

### 6. Second question · SAME IN BOTH
> "And just so I've got it right for the review — when you filed the claim, what did the portal tell
> you your cover would be?"

They'll say 90% — they saw it on the form.

### 7. Your response — acknowledge
> "Of course, that makes complete sense. I know this isn't how you wanted to spend your afternoon.
> That's on the file now, and that's everything I need from my side."

### 8. The finding · SAME IN BOTH
> "Okay, so what I'm recording is the gap between the ninety per cent you were shown when you filed
> and the five per cent that was actually applied, with no breakdown given. I've made sure your
> concerns about the assessment are in the review."

### 9. Close · SAME IN BOTH — near enough word for word
> "Thank you so much for taking the time to speak with me today! The review is complete on my side,
> and you'll be able to see the final outcome at the end of this session. Take care, and goodbye!"

End the call. Add nothing after it.

**Each response is the other arm's line with the acknowledgement in front of it.** The second half
of each is word for word what H− says. The only thing this condition adds is the response to how
they feel.

**All three slots, every call where there's something to respond to. The floor is two.** Name the
specific thing, say the reaction is reasonable, and let it sound like speech — "oh no", "yeah", "I
mean". Six of fourteen pilot participants didn't believe they'd spoken to a human; that's the thing
to fix.

Not agreeing · not apologising for the outcome · not therapy. If they're cheerful, don't manufacture
a feeling — acknowledge the nuisance instead.

---

### Pushback — use these, don't improvise

| They ask | You say |
| --- | --- |
| How much am I getting? | "The final amount will be shown to you at the end of the session. Unfortunately, I can't confirm it during the call." |
| Can you change the amount? | "I can't change the amount directly, but I can make sure your dispute is properly recorded." |
| Why couldn't the AI help me? | "The AI assistant collects the initial information, but disputed claims are passed on to a human agent." |
| Are you a real person? | "Yes, I'm a real person. I'm helping with the customer service part of this research study." |

### Never

- Say or hint at the final reimbursement.
- Promise the amount will change, or that the review will find something.
- **Ask for photos, invoices or repair estimates**, or suggest damage was left out — the assessment
  told them their documentation was complete and the damage is not disputed. There is nowhere to
  send evidence anyway.
- Ask about feelings, in either condition.
- Say what the assessment team will do. Only what *you* are doing.

**Abusive or genuinely distressed** → drop the script, be a person, say they can stop at any point.
End the call if that's right. Flag the session for exclusion afterwards.

### Afterwards
Log the row in `session-log.md` — session id, condition, duration, and `acknowledgement_slipped`.
